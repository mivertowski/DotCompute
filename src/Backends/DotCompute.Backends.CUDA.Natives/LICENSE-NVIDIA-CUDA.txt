NVIDIA CUDA Redistributable Runtime Libraries
=============================================

This package contains unmodified redistributable binaries from the NVIDIA CUDA
Toolkit (the CUDA Runtime "cudart" and the NVIDIA Runtime Compiler "NVRTC",
including its builtins library), obtained from NVIDIA's official redistributable
archive at https://developer.download.nvidia.com/compute/cuda/redist/ and
verified against the SHA-256 checksums published in NVIDIA's redistrib manifests.
